How to play

Guess a 6-letter word using up to one guess of each length 1 to 6, plus an extra final guess.

Each guess must be a valid word, except the first row, which can be any letter.

The colors of the tiles will show how close your guess was to the word.

Green means an exact match. Yellow means the letter is in the word, but in another spot. Gray means it is not in the word at all.

Arrows point you to other instances of the letter in the target word that haven't been matched yet.
