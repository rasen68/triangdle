How to play

Guess a 6-letter word using up to one guess of each length 1 to 6, in any order.

Each guess must be a valid word, except the first row, which can be any letter.

The colors of the tiles will show how close your guess was to the word.

  • G In the word once, in that spot.
  • Y In the word once, but to the left of that spot.
  • X Not in the word at all.
  • G In the word in that spot, and one more time to the right.

Click rows to choose the order of your guesses. Guessing the 6-letter row ends the game.
