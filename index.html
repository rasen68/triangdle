How to play

You have seven rows: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 6 letters. Play them in any order.

Rows 2–6 require a word from the matching dictionary. The one-letter row accepts any letter.

Green means exact. Gold means the letter exists elsewhere in the target. Gray means it is not available.

Arrows show other still-unclaimed copies of a matched letter in the target.

Your score is the total number of letters in submitted rows.
